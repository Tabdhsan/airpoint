What ports to the real app easily

  • Squishy, sinking and springy buttons (a custom button style with a spring and haptics)
  • Glows, gradients, stickers, LED meters, knobs and thumbwheels
  • Trails, confetti and doodle bursts (drawn with Canvas)
  • Most easter eggs: tap counters, hold-to-charge, gesture combos on the strips

What takes more work or gets cut

  • Holding two buttons at once and two-finger tap need custom touch handling (the app already has the latter)
  • A character like Biscuit: fine as layered shapes with a few poses; richer animation would need Rive
  • Chaos Zine's "boiling" lines and Arcade's Player 2 cursor are prototype-only gags
  • Sounds would ship as short audio clips rather than being synthesized
