    Secrets (spoilers)
    • CHAOS MODE: scribble left-right hard on the touchpad. Every sticker reshuffles its tilt and ink for a few seconds while confetti falls and the TV tiles wiggle. Typing chaos or party in Type also sets it off.
    • Unhinged status: tap the "Connected to…" line again and again. Each tap gets weirder, then it calms down by itself.
    • Falls apart: long-press the gear. Every sticker drops with gravity and bounces, then springs back into place. The hit areas never move.
    • Bad registration: tap the green status dot 5 times fast. The riso ink layers slide way out of register for a while.
    • Clicky: after 100 clicks a critter moves into the empty corner between the strips. Tap it. Clicks so far: 0.

    "Calm it down" (on by default if your system asks for reduced motion) turns off tilt, wobble, bursts and the line boil. Colours and layout stay the same.
