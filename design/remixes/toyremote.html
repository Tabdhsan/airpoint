Secrets (spoilers)
  1. Limited-edition shells. Spin any corner screw two full turns (drag in a circle on it, or tap it eight times). It pops and the faceplate swaps: Rubber Duck, Mint Soap, Grape Soda, Glow-in-the-Dark, then back to stock.
  2. Battery door. Hold Home and OK together for about a second (two thumbs; on a keyboard hold H+O). The touchpad pops off to reveal the batteries and the credits.
  3. Knob snaps off. Turn the speed knob to max (hare), then keep cranking. It strains, pops off, bounces down the remote and snaps back into its socket.
  4. Peel the sticker. Drag the QC sticker in the pad corner to peel it. Something is printed underneath. Tap the spot to put a fresh sticker on.
  5. Bonus: Turbo. Use the wheels for ▲▲▼▼◀▶◀▶ to rainbow the laser for a while. Also, the speaker grille honks.
