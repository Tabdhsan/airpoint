SECRETS (SPOILERS)
  1. KONAMI CODE: on the strips go up, up, down, down, left, right, left, right, then press OK. Turbo Mode turns on: the cabinet cycles through rainbow colours, the cursor becomes a spaceship and points count double. Enter the code again to turn it off.
  2. COIN SLOT: tap the little orange slot on the marquee. Each tap adds a credit. At 3 credits Player 2 joins with a mirrored cyan cursor.
  3. COMBO + HI-SCORE: every click scores. Quick clicks build a combo up to x8, and the 15th click posts a NEW HI-SCORE on the TV.
  4. SUPER OK: hold OK until the yellow ring fills (about 1.6s), then let go for a shockwave across the TV and +5000. A shorter hold is a normal long press.
