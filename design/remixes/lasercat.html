Secrets (spoilers)
  1. Pet Biscuit. Tap the cat: she purrs, wiggles and floats hearts. Five pets in a row earns a slow blink, which in cat means "I love you".
  2. Cat nap. Leave the remote alone for 15 seconds. She curls up with zzz and the Laser button reads "Laser (cat napping)". Any touch wakes her with a "mrrp?".
  3. Zoomies. Draw a fast circle on the touchpad. She does two laps of the pad, kicks up dust, and lands like nothing happened.
  4. Say the word. Type "meow" in the Type sheet. She answers, and every tile on the TV grows ears.
  5. Occupied. Try connecting to "Cardboard Box" in the TV menu.
  6. Small stuff. Hold the pad and she kneads biscuits. The Home icon has ears. The speed slider runs from loaf to zoomies, and the thumb is a ball of yarn.
